# Gemini prompts: My Garden art

Two sheets. For both, attach `design/gemini-relic-sheet.webp` (or the icon sheet) as the style reference so everything matches the game.

After you save them as `design/gemini-garden-plants.webp` and `design/gemini-garden-decor.webp`, cut them with:

```
node scripts/cut-icon-sheet.cjs design/gemini-garden-plants.webp garden
node scripts/cut-icon-sheet.cjs design/gemini-garden-decor.webp decor
```

## Sheet 1: 25 garden plants (5 × 5)

Create 25 garden plant illustrations for my calm mobile garden game Bloomline, in exactly the same style as the attached sheet: the same painterly-but-clean look, soft moss-and-parchment palette, light from the upper left, gentle outline and level of detail. These plants grow in the player's personal garden as a reward, so they should feel cosy, a little magical, and beautiful to look at for a long time.

**Layout (important, the image is cut by a script):**
- One square image, a grid of **5 columns × 5 rows**: 25 equal square cells.
- Every cell has the **same flat, plain, dark moss-green background** (#151D18): no texture, no gradient, no frame, no border, no grid lines, no shadow outside the plant.
- One whole plant per cell, **standing upright with its base at the bottom centre of the cell** (it will be placed on the ground), filling about 75% of the cell height, with clear empty space around it.
- No text, no letters, no numbers, no labels, no pots: the plants grow straight from a tiny tuft of grass.

**The 25 plants, in this exact order (left to right, top row first):**

Row 1, small flowers (low, delicate):
1. **Daisy**: white petals, golden centre, two or three stems.
2. **Clover**: a tuft of clover leaves with two soft pink-white clover flowers.
3. **Buttercup**: glossy butter-yellow cups on thin stems.
4. **Violet**: a low cluster of deep purple violets with heart-shaped leaves.
5. **Tulip**: one tall, closed, warm-red tulip.

Row 2, tall flowers:
6. **Iris**: blue-violet iris with sword-shaped leaves.
7. **Foxglove**: a tall spike of pink bell flowers.
8. **Lily**: a white-and-peach lily, open, elegant.
9. **Young Oak**: a small young oak tree, round crown, slim trunk.
10. **Lilac Bush**: a soft bush with lilac flower plumes.

Row 3, trees and rare Boss flowers:
11. **Rowan**: a slender tree with orange-red berry clusters.
12. **Cherry Tree**: a small tree in pale pink blossom.
13. **Silver Thistle**: a silvery thistle with a soft lavender crown.
14. **Queen Rose**: a deep red rose, regal, with a tiny golden glint.
15. **Desert Bloom**: a sandy-coloured succulent with one bright coral flower.

Row 4, rare Boss flowers:
16. **Moonflower**: a pale white trumpet flower with a faint moon-glow.
17. **Snowdrop**: drooping white snowdrops with a touch of frost.
18. **Hazel Bloom**: a hazel twig with yellow catkins and a little hazelnut.
19. **Cactus Flower**: a small round cactus crowned with a magenta flower.
20. **Twin Lily**: two identical lilies on one stem, mirrored.

Row 5, rare flowers:
21. **Path Poppy**: a bright orange poppy leaning as if along a path.
22. **Wayfarer Aster**: a cluster of sky-blue asters, airy and wind-blown.
23. **Mist Orchid**: a pale orchid half wrapped in soft mist.
24. **Coin Marigold**: golden marigolds with petals like little coins.
25. **Set Bloom**: a white, many-petalled star-shaped flower with a soft glow. It will be tinted to each theme's colour, so keep it mostly white and pale.

**Constraints:**
- Each plant must read clearly at 40 px: one strong silhouette.
- Trees and bushes (9–12) may fill a little more of the cell but must stay inside it.
- Calm and natural, never cartoonish or neon.

## Sheet 2: 8 garden decorations (4 × 2)

Create 8 garden decorations for the same game, in exactly the same style as the attached sheet. They appear as the player's garden grows, as gentle milestone rewards.

**Layout (important):**
- One wide image, landscape 2:1, a grid of **4 columns × 2 rows**: 8 equal square cells.
- The same flat, plain, dark moss-green background (#151D18) in every cell; no texture, frame, border, grid lines, text or labels.
- One object per cell, centred, sitting on its base at the bottom of the cell, about 70% of the cell.

**The 8 decorations, in this exact order (left to right, top row first):**
1. **Garden bench**: a small weathered wooden bench with moss on its legs.
2. **Pond**: a small round pond seen slightly from above, with two lily pads and a soft reflection.
3. **Lantern**: a little hanging paper lantern on a short wooden post, glowing warm orange.
4. **Flower arch**: a wooden garden arch covered in pink climbing roses.
5. **Stepping stones**: three flat round stones in a gentle curve, mossy edges.
6. **Bird bath**: a stone bird bath with a tiny bird drinking.
7. **Wind chime**: a small bamboo wind chime hanging from a branch.
8. **Garden gate**: a low, open wooden gate with a little latch, leading into the garden.

**Constraints:** readable at 48 px, cosy and calm, maximum 3 main colours each.
