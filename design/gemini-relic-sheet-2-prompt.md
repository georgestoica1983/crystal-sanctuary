# Gemini prompt: 8 new relic icons (rules v16)

Attach `design/gemini-relic-sheet.webp` (the first relic sheet) as the style reference, then paste the prompt below.

---

Create 8 more relic icons for my mobile garden game Bloomline, in exactly the same style as the attached relic sheet. They must look like they belong to the same set: same painterly-but-clean illustration style, the same soft moss-and-parchment palette, the same lighting from the upper left, the same gentle outline and the same level of detail.

**Layout (important, the image is cut by a script):**
- One wide image, landscape 2:1, laid out as a grid of **4 columns × 2 rows**: 8 equal square cells.
- Every cell has the **same flat, plain, dark moss-green background** (#151D18), with no texture, no gradient, no frame, no border, no grid lines and no shadow outside the object.
- One object per cell, centred, filling about 70% of the cell, with clear empty space around it.
- No text, no letters, no numbers and no labels anywhere.

**The 8 relics, in this exact order (left to right, top row first):**

1. **First Bloom.** A single tulip just opening, its first petal unfolding, with a tiny sparkle on the petal tip. Feeling: "the first one counts double".
2. **Thorn Chorus.** A small sprig of red thorn flowers arranged like a choir, with two soft musical notes floating above it. Warm red and coral.
3. **Bell Tower.** A tiny mossy stone bell tower with a golden bell swinging, and three soft rings of sound. Gold and stone grey.
4. **Acorn Gate.** A small round garden gate shaped like an acorn cap and arch, slightly open, with warm light behind it. Acorn brown and amber.
5. **Long Shadow.** A garden lantern or sundial casting one long, soft, violet shadow across moss, with a half moon behind it. Lavender and indigo.
6. **Bramble Berries.** A cluster of dark purple berries tangled on a thorny bramble stem: friendly berries, spiky stem. Purple and deep red.
7. **Soft Moss.** A soft, round cushion of green moss with a metal spike gently sinking into it, harmless, with a few dew drops. Fresh light green.
8. **Sunbeam Lens.** A small brass magnifying lens catching a sunbeam, splitting it into a soft golden-rainbow glint. Brass, pale gold and a hint of iridescence.

**Constraints:**
- Each icon must read clearly at 32 px: one strong silhouette, maximum 3 main colours.
- Calm and cosy, never aggressive. Rewards feel warm, not flashy.
- Keep each object fully inside its cell; nothing may touch or cross the cell edges.
