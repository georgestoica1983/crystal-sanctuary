# Message for testers

Copy the text below and send it with the game link. Put your own link where it says [link].

---

Hi! I'm making a small, calm puzzle game for phones called **Bloomline**, and I'd love for you to try it before anyone else.

**Play here:** [link]
It runs in your phone's browser. Nothing to install, no sign-up. Tip: add it to your home screen so it opens like an app.

**How it works:** draw one line from your companion to the gate. Every tile you pass adds to your score, and longer lines score far more. Thorns cost Life, so plan your path.

**What I'd like you to do:**
1. Play one full Run, until the garden rests. It takes about 15 to 25 minutes, and you can stop and come back later.
2. At the end, tap **Tell us what you thought**. It's a one-minute form. You can also find it any time in the menu (☰ → Feedback).

**Good to know:**
- The game records a few anonymous game facts, like how far you got and when you stopped, so I can see where it gets confusing. No names, no emails, no contacts, nothing personal.
- There are no ads and nothing to buy.
- Honest is best. If something was boring, confusing or annoying, I really want to hear it.

Thank you! 🌱
